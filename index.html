The Name of Yahwehשֵׁם יַהְוֶהየያህዌ ስም

We believe in the one true Elohim whose Name is Yahweh. His Name was intentionally removed from scripture over 6,000 times and replaced with "LORD." We restore and honor His Name as revealed to Moshe — because a name is not a title, and Yahweh declared His Name as a memorial forever.

"I am Yahweh — that is My Name." — Yeshayahu 42:8
📜

Torah is Eternalהַתּוֹרָה נִצְחִיתኦሪት ዘላለማዊ ናት

Yahweh's Torah was not abolished. Yeshua himself said he came not to destroy the Torah but to fulfill it — to make it complete. We walk in Torah obedience as the covenant lifestyle Yahweh commanded for all who love Him.

"Do not think I came to abolish the Torah." — Mattityahu 5:17
🕎

Yeshua the Messiahיֵשׁוּעַ הַמָּשִׁיחַዬሱዕ መሢሑ

We believe Yeshua — not "Jesus," a Greek/Latin rendering — was the promised Hebrew Messiah, a Torah-observant teacher from Natzeret. We follow his actual teachings as recorded in the Hebraic gospel tradition, not the Hellenized Roman version.

"I was sent only to the lost sheep of the house of Yisrael." — Mattityahu 15:24
✡️

Hebrew Roots, Not Greekשׁוֹרָשִׁים עִבְרִיִּיםየዕብራይስጥ ሥሮች

The entire Bible was written by Hebrews, for Hebrews, in a Hebrew cultural and linguistic context. The Greek church imposed foreign philosophical frameworks onto Hebrew scripture. We read the Word through Hebrew eyes, culture, and language.

"Salvation is from the Yehudim." — Yochanan 4:22
🌙

Biblical Holy Daysמוֹעֲדֵי יַהְוֶהየያህዌ ቅዱሳን ቀናት

We observe the seven Biblical Feasts of Yahweh as commanded in Vayikra 23 — Pesach, Chag HaMatzot, Yom HaBikkurim, Shavuot, Yom Teruah, Yom Kippur, and Sukkot. These are Yahweh's appointed times, eternal in their covenant significance.

"These are My appointed feasts." — Vayikra 23:2
🕯️

Seventh-Day Shabbatשַׁבָּת הַיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִיየሰባተኛ ቀን ሰንበት

We honor the Shabbat from Friday evening to Saturday evening as Yahweh commanded at Creation and reaffirmed in Torah. Shabbat was never changed to Sunday — that was a Roman decree under Constantine in 321 CE.

"Remember the Shabbat day to keep it holy." — Shemot 20:8
🦁

Identity of Yisraelזֶהוּת יִשְׂרָאֵלየእስራኤል ማንነት

We affirm the covenant identity of the Tribes of Yisrael. Our ministry acknowledges the lineage of the Tribe of Benyamin and the broader scattered house of Yisrael being gathered back to covenant in these last days.

"I will gather the remnant of Yisrael." — Mikah 2:12
📖

The Full Canonהַקָּנוֹן הַמָּלֵאሙሉ ቀኖና

We recognize the broader canon including the Ethiopian Orthodox Bible (81 books), the Book of Enoch, Jubilees, and the Dead Sea Scrolls as valuable ancient witnesses to the Hebrew faith — texts the Roman church deliberately excluded.

"All scripture is given by inspiration of Yahweh." — 2 Timotheos 3:16

Prayer is direct communication with Yahweh through Yeshua the Messiah. We call upon Yahweh by His true Name. We intercede for the congregation, for the scattered house of Yisrael, and for all seeking the ancient paths.
