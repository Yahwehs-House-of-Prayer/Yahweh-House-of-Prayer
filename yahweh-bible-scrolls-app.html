🌅 Morning — Raising of the Ark

וַיְהִי בִּנְסֹעַ הָאָרֹן וַיֹּאמֶר מֹשֶׁה קוּמָה יְהוָה וְיָפֻצוּ אֹיְבֶיךָ

The Shema

שְׁמַע יִשְׂרָאֵל יַהְוֶה אֱלֹהֵינוּ יַהְוֶה אֶחָד

"Hear, O Yisrael: Yahweh our Elohim, Yahweh is One. And you shall love Yahweh your Elohim with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength."

Devarim (Deuteronomy) 6:4–5 · The Foundation of Faith

Shabbat Candle Blessing

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יַהְוֶה

"Blessed are You, Yahweh our Elohim, King of the universe, who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to kindle the Shabbat light."

Fetha Nagast
ፍትሐ ነገሥት · Law of the Kings
Free
Ethiopia's ancient sacred law code — combining ecclesiastical and civil law. In use since the 15th century, it governed the Ethiopian Empire for 500 years. Declares the 81-book canon authoritative. Traces its roots to the 318 Fathers of Nicaea and preserves both Torah principles and early apostolic law.

Sinodos (Senodos)
ሲኖዶስ · Apostolic Canons of Ethiopia
Free
The earliest Ethiopian canon law collection, fixed by the 14th century. Contains four sets of Apostolic Canons and is the primary source for the 81-book Ethiopian Biblical canon. Preserves the most ancient Aksumite church tradition — predating Roman Catholic or Protestant canon decisions.
